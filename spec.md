App Idea: Digital “Care Circle” for Elderly Parents
Problem Statement
Many Nepali families have children who migrate abroad for education, employment, or other opportunities while their elderly parents continue to live in Nepal. Although communication through phone calls and messaging apps is possible, family members living abroad often struggle to coordinate and monitor their parents’ everyday needs. They may not know whether their parents have taken medication, attended medical appointments, travelled safely, or received help when they need it. In emergencies or situations requiring physical assistance, contacting a trusted person nearby can also be difficult.
Existing healthcare applications mainly focus on medical services and do not adequately address the day-to-day coordination between elderly parents, family members living abroad, and trusted people within the local community. Therefore, there is a need for a secure digital platform that connects elderly parents with their family members and verified local helpers to provide timely assistance and improve their overall safety and wellbeing.
Objective
The main objective of the proposed Digital “Care Circle” application is to develop a secure family and community coordination platform that enables Nepali families living abroad to remotely support and coordinate care for their elderly parents living in Nepal.
The specific objectives are to:
Enable family members living abroad to monitor important daily activities, such as medication reminders, appointments, and planned visits.
Connect elderly parents with verified and trusted local contacts who can provide assistance when family members are unavailable.
Coordinate practical support, such as hospital visits, transportation, grocery collection, or household assistance.
Provide notifications and updates to family members when important tasks are completed or assistance is required.
Improve emergency communication by allowing elderly parents or trusted contacts to quickly notify family members when help is needed.
Create a secure and trusted care network rather than relying on unknown or unverified strangers.
Reduce the difficulties faced by families living abroad in managing and supporting the everyday needs of their elderly parents in Nepal.
